## Principles
- AI-only: an animated 3D court, on-screen text, AI voiceover and generated product cards. No human on camera and no real match footage.
- Audience: club players around Playtomic 1.5 to 4.0, especially the ones who think they're better than they are. Hooks name the frustration (level, results, partner) and the video gives one fix.
- Playtomic levels are the spine. Only competitive matches change a level, "reliability" sets the size of each swing, and partners can gain different amounts from the same match ([source](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/43310980754193-How-the-Playtomic-level-system-works)).
- One idea per video, 15 to 30 s, and every caption ends with a question.

## Visual style
- 3D court (Remotion + Three.js). Players look like generic pros: athletic, in shorts, a t-shirt and trainers, with a padel racket. Never real players' likenesses or names. Team A is cyan and team B is red.
- A shot library (forehand, backhand, volley, bandeja, víbora, smash, lob, chiquita). Scenes say who hits what and when, and the swing lands on the frame the ball arrives.
- The camera is chosen per video together with Brandon: overhead tactics view, a ball follow-cam, or a cut between them.
- Kit Room uses a product-card template.
- Voiceover (ElevenLabs) is added only after the silent cut is approved.

## Chapters (each video carries a series tag, e.g. "Level Up #4")
1. **Level Up** (flagship): Playtomic levels, 2.0 to 4.0, beating the tennis player. Includes the **Partner Problems** sub-series.
2. **Shot School**: grip, ready position, volley, lob, back glass, bandeja vs smash, smash, víbora.
3. **Court Zones**: serve and return, net play, no-man's-land, the glass, corners, recovering after a lob.
4. **Myth or Fact**: a claim, a beat to guess, then the answer. Check every rules claim against the FIP rules before scripting.
5. **Kit Room**: rackets, balls, pressurisers, shoes, grips. These lead to affiliate links.

The full idea list per chapter is in the doc. Recurring formats: "What went wrong?" (answer in the next video), "Rate this rally", "Is this legal?", and recurring characters with our own made-up names (The Tennis Player, The Overconfident 4.0, The Partner Who Blames You).

## Findability
Each chapter gets its own colour and cover, a chapter hashtag (e.g. #PadelPerfectoLevelUp), a TikTok playlist, captions that point to the next part, and a chapter page on the Pages site (link in bio).

## Recycling
Only winners are recycled: re-posted after about 3 months with a new hook, visuals and voice, remixed into another format, or compiled into longer YouTube videos. A flop gets one retry with a new hook, then the topic is dropped.

## Tracking
Tag every video with chapter, format, camera, hook type, voice and visual version. Log views, average watch time, shares, saves and follows at 48 h and 7 d. Rank by watch time and shares first, then follows, then views. Change one thing at a time.

## Revenue
1. Now: Amazon affiliate links on a kit page of the Pages site, tagged #ad. Sign up when Kit Room is ready, because you need 3 sales in 180 days.
2. TikTok Shop affiliate once the follower minimum is met, plus brand and retailer affiliate schemes.
3. Own digital products (e.g. "2.0 to 3.0 in 8 weeks").
4. Sponsors, and later the app. No drop shipping.
