# Concept backlog

- Beginner: Where to stand when your partner serves
- Beginner: Aim at their feet, not their body, from the back
- Beginner: Serve deep toward the side glass, not the middle
- Intermediate: When to bandeja vs when to smash
- Intermediate: Recovering after the lob goes over you
- Intermediate: Follow your serve straight to the net
- Advanced: Reading the opponent's lob from their shoulders
- Advanced: The bajada: attacking a high ball off the back glass
